How to play

  • Tap to jump. Tap again in the air to double jump.
  • Toppings are worth 5 points each. A pizza slice is rare: 50 points and an extra life.
  • Land on top of broccoli or karela to squish it. Squish a few in a row for more points.
  • Jump over pans, rolling pins and pizza cutters. When a cutter flies at head height, stay on the ground.
  • Hit a golden box from below for a surprise. The golden chef hat protects you for 5 seconds, and the magnet pulls toppings to you.
  • Lose all three lives and you get one pizza question. Answer it right to keep running.

Common questions

There's no sound. Check that Sound is on in the game (title screen or pause menu), that your phone's volume is up, and that the silent switch is off.

My best score is gone. Scores and Pizza Cards are saved only on your device. Deleting the app erases them.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
