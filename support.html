Support

Need help with Crust Quest, found a bug, or have an idea? Email crustquest@proton.me. We aim to get back to you within a few days.

If something went wrong, it helps to mention your iPhone model, the level, and what happened just before.

How to play

  • Tap to jump. Tap again in the air to double jump. On a computer, press the space bar.
  • Each level is a pizza order. Collect the ingredients shown on the order ticket at the top left.
  • A karela is chasing you. Each hit lets it close in and drops 2 ingredients. Ingredients, cheese bits and squished veggies push it back.
  • Jump over hot pans, pots of hot sauce, rolling onions and low chillies. When something flies at head height, stay on the ground. A red "!" warns you first.
  • Land on broccoli and cauliflower to squish them. Squish a few in a row for more points.
  • Hit gold boxes from below for a surprise. The chef hat protects you for 5 seconds, and the magnet pulls things to you.
  • Finish the order, then get past the chef at the oven. Jump his low dough balls and stay down for the high ones.
  • If the karela catches you, answer a pizza question to escape. This works once per level.
  • Finish with no hits for 3 stars. The next pizza opens once you have at least 1 star.

Common questions

There's no sound. Check that sound is on in the game (title screen or pause menu), that your phone's volume is up, and that the silent switch is off.

My stars and best scores are gone. Stars, best scores and Pizza Cards are saved only on your device. Deleting the app erases them.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
