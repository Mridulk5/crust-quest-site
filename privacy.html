Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Crust Quest is a game made by Mridul Kumar, an independent developer. This policy covers the Crust Quest app for iPhone and the version you can play in a web browser.

The short version

Crust Quest does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game keeps on your device

To work properly, the game saves a few settings on your own phone or computer:

  • your stars and best score on each level
  • which Pizza Cards you have unlocked
  • whether sound is on or off
  • whether you have already seen the game's tips

This stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, erases it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, location or any other personal details.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising or analytics services.
  • We do not sell or share any information, because we do not collect any.

Children

Crust Quest is made for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

The web version

The website and browser version are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and protect its service. We do not get access to that information. GitHub's practices are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
